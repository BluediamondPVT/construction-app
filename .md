Department-wise Roles & Responsibilities
1 Marketing Department
Identify potential clients (builders, industries, warehouses, corporates).

Prepare company profile, brochures, and presentations.

Handle tender submissions, quotations, and negotiations.

Maintain client relationships and follow up for new projects.

Coordinate with estimation team for BOQ and costing inputs.

2 Drawing / Design Department
Prepare fire fighting system design as per IS/NFPA/NBC standards.

Generate AutoCAD/Revit drawings (layout, schematic, pump room, sprinkler, hydrant).

Prepare BOQ and technical specifications.

Revise drawings based on client/consultant feedback.

Provide “As-Built” drawings after project completion.

3 Project Department
Plan and schedule project execution.

Coordinate between office, client, and site team.

Ensure material procurement and delivery on time.

Monitor site progress and quality compliance.

Conduct testing, commissioning, and handover documentation.

Support billing team with work completion certificates.

4 Accounts Department
Maintain company accounts, ledgers, and financial records.

Handle vendor payments, client billing, and receivables.

Prepare monthly/quarterly financial reports.

Ensure compliance with taxation and statutory requirements.

Support management in budgeting and cost control.

5 Store Department
Manage inventory of pipes, fittings, pumps, valves, and consumables.

Issue materials to site as per requirement.

Maintain stock register and daily material usage records.

Coordinate with purchase department for replenishment.

Ensure safe storage and proper handling of equipment.

6 HR Department
Handle recruitment, onboarding, and employee records.

Prepare offer letters, appointment letters, and compliance documentation.

Manage payroll, attendance, and leave records.

Conduct training sessions on safety and technical skills.

Ensure employee engagement and grievance handling.

Support management in manpower planning.
